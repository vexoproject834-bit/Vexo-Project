01 — Problem & Paradigm Shift

Keep the matrix. Don't isolate one component.

Water plants generate massive sludge waste while air purification relies on costly synthetic catalysts. VEXO tests whether native sludge constituents work synergistically as-is.

CONVENTIONAL PATHWAY • HIGH COST

Single-Component Extraction

  • ✕ Chemically separate one target metal or mineral.
  • ✕ Energy-intensive purification & secondary waste.
  • ✕ Rebuild an artificial catalyst with costly dopants.
  • ✕ Loses natural synergistic interactions between minerals.
VEXO HYPOTHESIS • CIRCULAR & LOW-BURDEN

Whole-Matrix Valorization

  • ✓ Retain native matrix: Use whole sludge directly.
  • ✓ Evidence-led engineering: Wash & granulate only as needed.
