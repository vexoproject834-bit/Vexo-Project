01 — The Dual Challenge

Two Environmental Crises, One Link

  • ●
    Ground-Level Ozone (O₃): A toxic secondary pollutant harming respiratory health and crops. Commercial filters rely on expensive synthetic catalysts.
  • ●
    Water-Treatment Sludge: Municipal plants continuously generate solid sludge requiring costly handling and hazardous disposal.
02 — VEXO Hypothesis

The Whole-Matrix Principle

Instead of using costly chemicals to extract a single metal, VEXO retains the native multicomponent sludge matrix. Natural iron oxides, manganese dioxide, and aluminosilicates work synergistically to capture and decompose ozone.

03 — Our Innovation

Keep the Matrix. Don't Isolate One Component.

❌ Conventional Extraction Approach

  • • Acid leaching & chemical extraction of single metals
  • • High energy consumption and secondary chemical waste
  • • Requires expensive synthetic supports (e.g., CeO₂)
  • • High production cost limits real-world scalability
VEXO NOVELTY

✅ VEXO Whole-Matrix Approach

  • • Preserves native Qena sludge composition intact
  • • Utilizes natural Fe₂O₃–MnO₂–SiO₂ synergistic interfaces
  • • Simple physical washing & granulation engineering
  • • Ultra-low cost circular economy air purification
