01 — Problem & Paradigm Shift

Keep the matrix. Don't isolate one component.

Water plants generate massive sludge waste while air purification relies on costly synthetic catalysts. VEXO tests whether native sludge constituents work synergistically as-is.

CONVENTIONAL PATHWAY High Cost & Waste

Single-Component Extraction

  • ✕ Select and chemically separate one target metal or mineral.
  • ✕ Energy-intensive purification and secondary waste generation.
  • ✕ Rebuild an artificial catalyst from scratch.
  • ✕ Loses natural synergistic interactions between native minerals.
VEXO HYPOTHESIS Circular & Low-Burden

Whole-Matrix Valorization

  • ✓ Retain native matrix: Utilize whole groundwater-treatment sludge directly.
  • ✓ Evidence-led engineering: Modify only when characterization justifies it.
